# Olist E-Commerce Business Performance Dashboard

A 5-page BI dashboard analyzing the Olist Brazilian e-commerce marketplace dataset, covering sales, logistics, customer satisfaction, and retention.

## Business questions answered
- How is revenue trending over time, and how is it distributed geographically?
- How well is the business meeting delivery-time and on-time-delivery targets?
- What drives customer satisfaction (or dissatisfaction), and which categories/states underperform?
- How does the business perform on customer retention and seller geographic coverage?

## Dataset
[Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce) (Kaggle) — orders, customers, sellers, products, reviews, and payments across Brazil, 2016–2018.
> Raw data not included in this repo — download directly from Kaggle using the link above.

## What's inside
Five dashboard pages:
1. **Executive Overview** — revenue trend, geographic revenue map, top cities by orders
2. **Sales & Revenue Analysis** — revenue vs. target, top categories by revenue, payment type/installments
3. **Delivery & Logistics Performance** — delivery time vs. target, approval/processing/shipping time breakdown, late deliveries by state/category
4. **Customer Satisfaction** — review scores vs. target, positive/negative review rates, top categories by review score
5. **Customer Retention & Geography** — new vs. returning customers, orders by customer location, seller geographic coverage

## Key numbers
| Metric | Value |
